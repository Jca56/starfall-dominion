# Gameplay Mechanics
Collect and manage resources, build armies to defend and secure new planets.

## Turn Based Strategy
- Do things on your turn, end turn, enemy does things, your turn again!

## Win Conditions
- Liberation - Pushed back the Starfall Dominion.
        Conditions
                - Secure the Dominion home planet
                - The Dominion do not own any other planets
- Technological Supremacy (Concept not locked in)
        Conditions:
                - Research the "Multidimensional Travel" technology
                - Build the "Dimension Portal" building
                - Survive until the Dimension Portal is fully charged
                - Hop to a safe alternate dimension or send the Dominion to another dimension 
## The Map
- Player starts on the far left/West of the map. Enemy AI starts on the far right/East.
- Divided into sectors.
 - Owning the majority of planets in a sector grants influnce over that sector.
- Fog of War
- Key locations are predetermined and the same every game
- Proc Gen planets and POIs fill in the rest of the map
- Control fleets that fly around the map

        ### Fleet Movement
        - Left-click selects a fleet.
        - The preview follows the cursor, capped at remaining movement.
        - Right-click commits the move to that endpoint.
        - The fleet completes the order, even if enemies become visible en route.
        - Distance traveled is deducted; leftover movement can fund another order that turn.

## Resources
- 3-5 different types
- Used for building and upgrading armies, researching new technologies, and growing the population.
- Resources are generated on planets
